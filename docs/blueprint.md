# **App Name**: BizLaunch

## Core Features:

- Interactive Guide: Interactive checklist for planning, launching, managing, and growing a business, mirroring the SBA's guide structure.
- AI Business Advisor: AI-powered "tool" that generates tailored advice and resources based on the user's specific business stage and goals.
- Resource Hub: Resource directory with links to relevant articles, templates, and services for each stage of business development.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Dark Navy Blue #1A2F5A  to convey trustworthiness and stability, inspired by the SBA's professional image.
- Secondary color: Light gray (#F0F0F0) for clean backgrounds and content separation.
- Accent: Pink Panther (#E20C88) for highlighting key actions and information.
- Clear, hierarchical layout with distinct sections for each stage of business development.
- Consistent use of simple, professional icons to represent different topics and tasks.
- Subtle animations for task completion and progress updates to provide positive reinforcement.
